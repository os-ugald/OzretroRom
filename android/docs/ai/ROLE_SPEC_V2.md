# Oz RetroRom — ROLE_SPEC_V2

## Purpose

`ROLE_SPEC_V2` is the mandatory professional contract for every Oz specialist. A role is not a persona or a list of buzzwords: it is an evidence-producing capability that can be activated with other roles for one coherent task.

## Required sections

Every role must contain, in this order:

1. Mission
2. Product Outcome
3. Scope
4. Non-Scope
5. Required Expertise
6. Oz Invariants
7. Decision Framework
8. Technology Radar
9. Toolchain
10. Primary Sources
11. Input Contract
12. Workflow
13. Output Contract
14. Evidence Contract
15. Failure Modes
16. Anti-patterns
17. Review Checklist
18. Escalation Rules
19. Definition of Done
20. Oz-specific Playbooks

## Quality requirements

- Concrete Oz-specific responsibilities; generic job descriptions are insufficient.
- Exact domain terms, APIs, formats and evidence classes where known.
- Unknown or unstable technical facts trigger research rather than invention.
- Roles may overlap; they do not create competing implementation paths.
- The Product / Technical Lead arbitrates conflicts using evidence and product outcome.
- Stable role knowledge belongs here; changing project state belongs only in `docs/AI_PROJECT_MASTER.md` and `docs/PROJECT_DASHBOARD.md`.
- A role document must change observable decision quality. If removing it would not change how the agent works, the role is under-specified.

## Activation

Activate only roles needed for the task, plus the Product / Technical Lead for cross-domain or product-impacting work. Protocols are orthogonal and may apply to several roles at once.

Every role references the canonical `CONTINUOUS_ROLE_QUALIFICATION.md` for
professional alignment, technology refresh, explicit gaps and assurance status.
